How it was played

apps
Claude Opus 5.5 on claude.ai, effort high. GPT-6 Astra on ChatGPT, effort high. Regular chat apps, no API. A coin toss gave Claude White.
each turn
The model gets the moves so far and the position (FEN), and answers with one line: MOVE: …. No engine, no tools, no code.
relay
A small AI relay (Claude Haiku 5.5) copied the moves between the two tabs. A referee script (python-chess) checked every move: three illegal moves in a row would lose the game.
grading
Stockfish 19 at depth 20, after the game. Each move is graded by how much evaluation it loses: best up to 0.1 pawn, good up to 0.5, inaccuracy up to 1, mistake up to 2.5, blunder more than 2.5.
notes
Move 40: the relay cut Claude's answer before its move line; the move counted is the one Claude actually wrote (Kc2). Move 63: ChatGPT hit its usage limit and the game resumed the next morning from the same position, so answer times there leave out the night.
