$ man lfm2.5-24b-a2b

When to pick it

pick it if
You want quick results on simple jobs: it built a working bakery site in about 4 minutes, almost twice as fast as Qwen3.6.
watch out
It broke on the harder tests: its Snake never redraws the screen, and its car crash shows a few floating bricks with a counter stuck at 0 after 18 minutes. On our 12 GB card it was not 1.7× faster: same writing speed as Qwen3.6.
