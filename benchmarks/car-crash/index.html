Car crash (2D physics)

Pi 1.1.0 coding agent (read, bash, edit, write), one prompt, no help, 20 min limit. Every model gets exactly this prompt:

Create a fun 2D physics animation drawn on a canvas, in the current folder (index.html plus any CSS and JavaScript files you need). A car drives from the left to the right and crashes into a brick wall made of at least 40 bricks. On impact, the bricks fly in all directions with gravity, bounce on the ground and pile up; the car gets damaged: its front crumples and some parts break off, with sparks or smoke. Show a counter "Bricks knocked: N" that goes up as bricks fall, and a "Replay" button that restarts the whole scene from the beginning. The animation starts by itself when the page opens. No external resources: no libraries, no images, no web fonts. When you are finished, reply DONE.
